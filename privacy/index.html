Privacy & cookies

This website belongs to David M. Baylis, an author based in the UK. I’m responsible for any personal information collected here. The short version: I collect as little as I can, I never sell it, and you can ask me to delete it at any time.

What I collect and why

  • Newsletter. If you sign up to Off the Record, I collect your email address (and your name, if the form asks for it) so I can send you the free chapters and occasional news about new books. I rely on your consent for this. Every email has an unsubscribe link. The list is run by MailerLite, which stores the list and sends the emails on my behalf.
  • Analytics. Only if you click “Accept” on the cookie banner, Google Analytics records things like how many people visit, which pages they read, roughly where they are (country or city), what device and browser they use, and which site sent them here. It uses cookies to do this. If you decline, it doesn’t run.
  • Clap button. The clap button on blog posts is provided by Lyket. It keeps a small record in your browser so the count stays honest. You don’t need an account, and it doesn’t know who you are.
  • Emails to me. If you email me at author@davebaylis.com, I keep your message so I can reply.
  • Hosting. This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may log technical information such as IP addresses for security purposes.

Changing your mind about cookies

You can change your cookie choice at any time using the cookie settings link here or at the bottom of any page.

Who else sees it

Only the services named above, and only so they can do the job described. Some of them may store data outside the UK. Where they do, they are required to protect it to the standard UK data protection law expects.

How long I keep it

Newsletter details are kept until you unsubscribe. Analytics data is used only in aggregate reports, and I don’t use it to identify anyone. Emails are kept for as long as the conversation is useful.

Your rights

You can ask to see the information I hold about you, have it corrected or deleted, or object to how it’s used. Email author@davebaylis.com and I’ll sort it out.

If you’re unhappy with how I’ve handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk. I’d appreciate the chance to put it right first.
