Audiobook Giveaway Terms

Last updated: 11 November 2026

The short version

If you live in the US or UK, sign up to the newsletter by the end of 10 December 2026 and you’re in a free draw. Three people win an audiobook. It costs nothing, and buying a book won’t improve your chances.

1. Who’s running it

The giveaway is run by David M. Baylis (“the promoter”). Contact: author@davebaylis.com.

2. Who can enter

The giveaway is open to residents of the United States and the United Kingdom aged 18 or over. It is void where prohibited. The promoter and members of his household can’t enter.

Anyone outside the US and UK is welcome to sign up to the newsletter but can’t be entered into the draw, because the audiobook codes only work in those two countries. If you win, you may be asked to confirm which country you live in.

3. How to enter

Sign up to the newsletter using the form at davebaylis.com between 11 November 2026 and 23:59 GMT on 10 December 2026. Signing up is your entry.

One entry per person and per email address. Duplicate or automated sign-ups won’t count.

No purchase necessary. Buying a book does not increase your chance of winning.

4. The prize

There are three winners. Each receives one audiobook of [TITLE], delivered as an Audible promotional code for their country: Audible US (audible.com) for US winners, Audible UK (audible.co.uk) for UK winners. You will need an Audible account in that country to redeem it.

Codes are limited for each country. Prizes go to winners in the order they are drawn, and if no code is left for a winner’s country, another winner will be drawn instead.

The prize has no cash alternative and can’t be transferred, though the promoter may substitute a prize of similar value if the original becomes unavailable.

5. How winners are picked

Three winners will be drawn at random from all eligible entries within seven days of the closing date. The odds depend on how many people enter.

6. Telling the winners

Winners will be emailed at the address they signed up with. If a winner hasn’t replied within 14 days, a replacement winner will be drawn. Please check your spam folder.

7. Your details

Email addresses are used to run the newsletter and this draw, as set out in the privacy notice. You can unsubscribe at any time. If you unsubscribe before the draw, you won’t be entered. Winners’ names won’t be published without their permission.

8. The small print

The promoter may cancel or change the giveaway if circumstances outside his control require it. Anyone who breaks these terms or tries to game the draw will be disqualified. This giveaway is not sponsored, endorsed or run by Amazon, Audible, or any social media platform. These terms are governed by the law of England and Wales.
