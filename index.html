About

Beyond the neon

There’s a version of Las Vegas that exists in the imagination — louder than the real one, stranger, more desperate — and David M. Baylis has been living there for years.

A career in data gave him a useful education in the gap between how things appear and how they actually are. His fiction lives in that gap, out on the roads where the neon runs out.

He is based in the UK. He writes whenever the opportunity presents itself, which is never quite enough.
